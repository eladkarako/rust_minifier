Line preserving minifier.

Used for formats where newlines and indentation are part of the syntax: YAML, shell, INI/properties, Makefiles, Dockerfiles, SQL-like config files and many scripting languages. Comments are removed first, trailing whitespace is trimmed, blank lines are dropped, and the line structure is otherwise kept intact. Heredocs and YAML block scalars are preserved verbatim so their contents are never altered.
